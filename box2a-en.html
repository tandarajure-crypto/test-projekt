JURE’S BRANCH

Jure Tandara and Matija née Lekić

Jure Tandara: ★ c. 1725  –  ✝ unknown

Wife Matija née Lekić: ★ unknown  –  ✝ unknown

Jure Tandara, born c. 1725, was the eldest son of Ivan Tandara. Jure’s branch is the oldest branch of the Tandara family.

Jure and Matija had a son, Frane, and daughters Anđa and Ana. Frane married Boža née Ćosić, and their son Nikola was born in 1792.

No member of Jure’s family was recorded in the 1806 census of Ričice. Because information about this branch is missing, it is possible that the family moved away from Ričice, after which every trace of its members and descendants was lost.

It would be valuable if a descendant of this branch were one day to make contact and confirm belonging to Jure’s line.
