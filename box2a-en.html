JURE'S BRANCH

Jure Tandara and Matija née Lekić

Jure Tandara: ★ about 1725  –  ✝ unknown

Wife Matija née Lekić: ★ unknown  –  ✝ unknown

They had a son, Frane, and daughters Anđa and Ana. Frane and Boža née Ćosić had a son Nikola, born in 1792. The diagram below contains all coded members of this branch.

Instructions for using the diagram

  1. Enter a person’s name or code and press Find.
  2. The + appears only where the surname line continues; terminal people have no +/− marker.
  3. Spouses and daughters are displayed vertically below the surname carrier; sons are displayed horizontally to the right.
  4. Search zooms to the found person and keeps the parent connector attached.
