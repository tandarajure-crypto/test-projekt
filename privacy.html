Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the private Digital Archive of the Tandara Family portal, available only to authorized users.

1. Purpose of the Website

The website has been created to preserve the history, genealogy, migration records, family heritage and historical information related to the Tandara family line.

2. Information You May Voluntarily Provide

Through the contact form you may voluntarily submit your name, surname, place of residence, country, e-mail address, relationship to the Tandara family, photographs, historical documents, family records or any other information you choose to share.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Submitted information is used solely for responding to inquiries, verifying genealogical information, expanding the family archive, evaluating historical materials and communicating with contributors.

Personal information is never sold, rented or used for unsolicited marketing communications.

4. Public and Private Genealogical Information

The public archive includes confirmed deceased family members and people born in 1930 or earlier who, based on their age and family context, can reasonably be regarded as deceased.

A person born after 1930 also remains public when their death has been confirmed. A person without a known birth year may remain public when parents, siblings, spouse or children reliably place that person in an older deceased generation.

Names, birth years, current residence and family details of living people, as well as people born after 1930 whose year of death has not been entered or confirmed, are removed from public view. Only anonymous genealogical numbers may remain in the public view as internal markers showing that a person's record exists and is linked to the appropriate position in the family tree. These numbers do not themselves contain a name, date of birth, place of residence or contact information and do not provide access to the private record. Access to protected information is not permitted without valid prior approval from the administrator of the separate private archive. Email verification alone does not grant access: every request is reviewed and approved manually.

Photographs of such people that have already appeared in publicly accessible online interviews may remain publicly displayed in a neutral context, without names or genealogical links, together with a clear privacy notice. View the publicly published photographs.

If you believe that published information is inaccurate, outdated or should not appear on this website, you may request correction, restriction or removal.

5. Contact Form and FormSubmit

The contact form uses the FormSubmit service to deliver submitted messages to the website administrator by e-mail.

The form includes several anti-spam mechanisms, including CAPTCHA verification, a hidden honeypot field and blacklist protection against automated spam submissions.

6. Website Analytics

The private family archive does not use Umami, FlagCounter or other visitor analytics tools.

7. Interactive Maps and External Sources

The interactive migration map remains enabled because it is a functional part of the family archive and migration-route display.

The private site permits only the Leaflet/OpenStreetMap resources required for the map. Analytics trackers and indexing remain disabled, and map requests use a no-referrer policy.

8. Cookies and Private Access

The private portal does not use analytics cookies. It uses only a necessary security session for authentication, approval checks and protection of private content.

9. Private Portal Hosting

The private portal must be hosted behind real access control (login/key or another authentication method). The hosting provider may retain technical security and access logs that cannot be disabled by HTML code alone.

10. Security Measures

The private version uses noindex/nofollow, no-referrer, Content Security Policy (CSP), blocking of automatic external network connections, mixed-content protection, SAMEORIGIN protection at server level where supported, and no-store caching rules. These measures supplement, but do not replace, private-portal authentication.

11. Your Rights

You may request information regarding personal data relating to you, correction of inaccurate information, removal of information, restriction of publication or withdrawal of previously granted consent.

12. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests concerning correction or removal of personal information, please use the Contact & Collaboration page.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to improve security, reflect changes to the website or comply with applicable legal or technical requirements.
