THE SECOND BRANCH OF THE FIRST TANDARA FAMILY

2.0. Petar Tandara (1734–1817)

Petar Tandara was Ivan’s second son. He died in 1817 at the age of eighty-three, so it is estimated that he was born around 1734. Petar was married to Jela, née Budić. They had seven sons—Grgo, Jakov (1773–), Martin, Marijan (1781–), Mijo, Tadija and Marko (1787–)—and two daughters, Manda (1777–) and Jela (–1824). The sons Jakov (1773–), Marijan (1781–), Tadija and Marko (1787–) do not appear in the 1806 Register of Souls, and Tadija is also absent from the 1819 register. It may therefore be assumed that they were no longer alive or that one of them had moved away from Ričice.

2.1. Grgo, son of Petar (1770–)

Grgo, Petar’s eldest son, married Ruža, née Pušić. They had a son, Šimun, and a daughter, Iva (1798–).

2.1.1. Šimun, son of Grgo (1800–1874)

Šimun was married to Jela, née Budimir, with whom he had a son, Mate (1826–), and three daughters: Iva (1831–), Matija (1837–) and Marta (1845–). Only Mate’s year of birth is known, so his later life and the circumstances explaining the absence of further records cannot be established. Šimun’s family line ended after his son Mate.

2.2. Martin, son of Petar (1775–1844)

Martin, Petar’s third son, married Ruža, née Lekić. Between the 1806 and 1819 censuses, Martin moved with his family from Ričice to Zavelim, together with Ante Antin Strčević. They had six sons—Ivan, Jure (1811–), Mijo, Grgo, Filip (1821–) and Josip (1824–)—and two daughters, Matija and Ana (1828–). No records of Jure, Filip or Josip have been found apart from their baptismal entries, so it is not known whether they died young or moved away. Martin’s son Mijo left no male descendants, so the family line was continued only by Martin’s sons Ivan and Grgo.

2.2.1. Ivan, son of Martin (1807–1877)

Ivan, Martin’s eldest son, married Antica, née Lažeta, in 1826. Ivan and Antica had three sons—Marijan (1828–1828), Toma and Jure (1840–)—and four daughters: Ruža (1834–), Iva, Joza (1839–1839) and Matija (1841–). No information about Jure has been found apart from the record of his birth. Toma was the only successor to Ivan’s line and the founder of the branch known by the nickname TROMIĆI.

2.2.2. Mijo, son of Martin (1814–)

Martin’s third son and his wife Matija, née Parlov, had eight children: two sons, Martin (1850–) and Mijo (1851–1851), and six daughters—Lucija (1836–1836), Manda (1838–1838), Boža (1839–), Joza (1845–), Marija (1852–) and Matija (1856–). Mijo died shortly after birth, while no records of Martin have been found apart from the record of his birth. It is not known whether he died or moved away. Mijo’s line ended after Martin.

2.2.3. Grgo, son of Martin (1818–)

Grgo, son of Martin, married twice. From his first marriage to Rosa, née Budimir, he had a son, Lovro, and a daughter, Jaka (1845–). In his second marriage to Jaka, née Knežević, Grgo had two sons, Mate (1853–1856) and Mijo, and three daughters: Matija (1850–), Joza (1861–1878) and Veronika (1866–).

2.3. Mijo, son of Petar (dates unknown)

Mijo, Petar’s fifth son, married Ruža, whose surname is unknown. They had only one daughter, Matija (1810–). Mijo had no successor to continue his family line. In 1819, the family of Martin Tandara, Lovro’s grandfather, was living in Zavelim. Martin’s sons Ivan, Mijo and Grgo were listed there. When the Austrian cadastral survey was conducted in 1835, Martin Tandara, son of the late Petar, was recorded as a property owner in Ričice. At that time, most clans from Ričice owned property in Ričice, Zavelim, Podbila and Vir. These villages belonged to the same parish in Ričice. Many families within the same clan moved from one locality to another, even within Ričice itself. The population was growing rapidly and new living space was needed. In this context, it is understandable that a family, or part of a family, could be recorded in Ričice in one register and in Zavelim in the next, or vice versa. It is known with certainty, however, that Grgo returned with his children from Zavelim to Ričice, where he continued living with his family.

TROMIĆI

2.2.1.1. Toma, son of Ivan (1830–)

Toma was married to Tomica, née Kovač. They had four sons: Ivan (1866–), Mate (1870–), Jure and another son also named Ivan (1879–1879). Toma’s only successor was his son Jure. Toma’s line in Zavelim was known by the nickname TROMIĆI, which, according to family tradition, referred to their tall stature and slow, heavy gait.

THE LINE OF JURE, SON OF TOMA

2.2.1.1.1. Jure, son of Toma (1874–1931)

Toma’s third son and only successor, Jure, married Iva, née Lažeta. They had five sons—Toma (1909–1914), Ivan (1911–1915), Pio, Dominik and Mate—and a daughter, Matija (1908–). The line was continued by the sons Pio, Dominik and Mate.

2.2.1.1.1.1. Pio, son of Jure (1916–1990)

Pio, Jure’s third son, married Terezija, née Budimir. They had three sons—Ivan, Jure and Jozo—and a daughter, Mara (1937–).

2.2.1.1.1.1.1. Ivan, son of Pio (1935–2025)

Ivan was married to Marijana, née Petter, with whom he had a son, Dino, and a daughter, Romana. The family now lives in Zagreb.

2.2.1.1.1.1.1.1. Dino, son of Ivan (1964–)

Dino married Matilda, née Klepikapić. They have three daughters: Hedviga (1991–), Leonarda (1995–) and Lucija (1999–). He has no male descendants. The family lives in Zagreb.

2.2.1.1.1.1.2. Jure, son of Pio (1943–2004)

Jure was married to Mila, née Škorić. They had a son, Tomislav (1972–), and three daughters: Marija (1968–), Angela (1971–) and Nediljka (1972–). Tomislav never married. The family lives in Stobreč.

2.2.1.1.1.1.3. Jozo, son of Pio (1949– )

Jozo married Mirjana, née Generalić. They have a son, Domagoj (1978–), and two daughters, Tereza (1973–) and Zrinka (1975–). The family lives in Split.

2.2.1.1.1.2. Dominik, son of Jure (1919–1987)

Dominik, Jure’s fourth son, married Mara, née Knezović. They had six sons—Ante (1948–), Bariša (1950–), Nikola (1953–), Pio (1954–), Sveto (1958–) and Ivan (1963–)—and a daughter, Olga (1965–). Ante, Nikola, Pio and Sveto have no sons. No information about Bariša’s marriage is available. Only Ivan’s sons continued Dominik’s line.

2.2.1.1.1.2.1. Ante, son of Dominik (1948– )

Ante was married to Dubravka, née Pencinger. They had two daughters, Ana Marija (1978–) and Ozana (1982–). The family lives in Križevci.

2.2.1.1.1.2.2. Bariša, son of Dominik (1950– )

Bariša Dominikov is married to Nada (surname unknown), and they have no children.

2.2.1.1.1.2.3. Nikola, son of Dominik (1953– )

Nikola married Nada, née Jurčević. They had four daughters: Marijana (1974–), Iva (1975–), Brigita (1979–) and Anita (1982–). After returning, Nikola once again lives in the village of Tandare in Zavelim.

2.2.1.1.1.2.4. Pio, son of Dominik (1954– )

Pio married Ljubina, née Krinulović. They had two daughters, Tina and Pia. The family lives in Vienna, Austria.

2.2.1.1.1.2.5. Sveto, son of Dominik (1958–)

Sveto married twice. In his first marriage to Petra, née Lukić, he had a daughter, Daniela (1982–). In his second marriage to Vera, née Rakin, he had a daughter, Eni (1997–).

2.2.1.1.1.2.6. Ivan, son of Dominik (1963– )

Ivan married Mirjana Tandara. They have two sons, Dominik (1990–) and David. The family lives in the village of Tandare in Zavelim.

2.2.1.1.1.3. Mate, son of Jure (1922–1963)

Mate, Jure’s fifth son, married Mara, née Budić. They had two sons, Tomislav and Mijo (1955–1960), and a daughter, Matija (1952–).

2.2.1.1.1.3.1. Tomislav, son of Mate (1947–2014)

Tomislav married Božica, née Golubiček. They have a son, Mate, and a daughter, Martina (1978–). The family moved to Zagreb.

2.2.1.1.1.3.1.1. Mate, son of Tomislav (1980– )

Mate married Ivana, née Trošelj. They had a daughter, Tena (2006–). The family now lives in Zagreb.

THE LINE OF LOVRO, SON OF GRGO

2.2.3.1. Lovro, son of Grgo (1842–1922)

Lovro married Iva, née Parlov. They had three sons—Ante, Ivan (1876–) and Marijan—and three daughters: the first Lucija (1864–1876), Iva (1884–1908) and the second Lucija (–1877). Ante and Marijan were the only successors to the line. Their descendants had thirty-three children. The death register of the Ričice parish records that Lovro’s daughter Luca was “killed by rocks” at the age of thirteen.

2.2.3.1.1. Ante, son of Lovro (1871–1933)

Ante was married to Tomica, née Lukić. They had five sons—Ivan, Marijan, the first Mate (1906–1907), the second Mate (1910–) and Nikola (1915–1981)—and three daughters: Marta (1901–), Iva (1905–1911) and Matija (1908–). The successors to the line were Ivan, Marijan, Mate and Nikola.

2.2.3.1.1.1. Ivan, son of Ante (1896– )

Ante’s eldest son, Ivan, married Mara, née Mršić. They had five sons—Lovro, Mirko, Mate, Ante Vlade and Nikola (1941–)—and a daughter, Tomica (1930–). Ivan moved to Slatina, and his descendants now live in Predavac near Bjelovar, Slatina, Obrovac, Zagreb and Germany.

2.2.3.1.1.2. Marijan, son of Ante (1898–1933)

Marijan was married to Milica, née Mikulić. They had two sons, Frane (1927–1945), who was killed in Vienna, and Ante (1929–1929), as well as two daughters, Marija (1930–) and Stana (1932–1933). Marijan died of pneumonia at the age of thirty-five. His sons had no descendants, so his line ended.

2.2.3.1.1.3. Mate, son of Ante (1910–1991)

Mate, Ante’s son, was married to Mara, née Knežević. They had two sons, Ante Bruno and Frane, and three daughters: Ana (1935–), Dinka (1945–) and Olga (1952–).

2.2.3.1.1.4. Nikola, son of Ante (1915–1981)

Nikola was married to Tomica, née Budimir. They had four sons—Ante (1950–1950), Drago, Jerko and Ivan (1955–1955)—and three daughters: Matija (1944–), Branka (1947–) and Marija (1948–). Jerko was married to Stanka Racetin, with whom he had a daughter, Ana (1985–). Drago is the only successor to the line, and his sons now live in Ričice.

2.2.3.1.1.1.1. Lovre, son of Ivan (1925– )

Lovre, Ivan’s eldest son, married Rozika, née Peška. They had two daughters, Štefica (1955–) and Ana (1967–). The family lives in Slatina.

2.2.3.1.1.1.2. Mirko, son of Ivan (1928– )

Mirko married Danica, née Lukić. They had two sons, Ivan and Veselko, and a daughter, Mirjana (1957–). The family lives in Predavac near Bjelovar.

2.2.3.1.1.1.2.1. Ivan, son of Mirko (1961– )

Mirko’s elder son, Ivan, married Nada, née Kegalj. They had two sons, Robert and Kristijan. The family lives in Predavac near Bjelovar.

2.2.3.1.1.1.2.2. Veselko, son of Mirko (1964– )

Mirko’s younger son, Veselko, married Martina, née Höhmann. They have a son, Mirko, and a daughter, Janina. The family lives in Predavac.

2.2.3.1.1.1.3. Mate, son of Ivan (1932–1970)

Ivan’s third son married Marija Srebrnjak. They had only one daughter, Sandra (1967–).

2.2.3.1.1.1.4. Ante Vlade, son of Ivan (1934– )

Ivan’s fourth son, Ante Vlade, married twice. In his first marriage to Karina, née Mesch, he had two sons, Johan and Franz, and a daughter, Veronika. He had no children in his second marriage to Tonka, née Zanne. The family moved to Obrovac.

2.2.3.1.1.1.4.1. Johan, son of Ante Vlade (1960– )

Johan and his wife Katarina, née Köniks, have a son, Gerd, and a daughter, Medita. The family lives in Germany.

2.2.3.1.1.1.4.2. Franz, son of Ante Vlade (1966– )

Franz married Honolore, née Genesen. They have a son, Marvin. The family lives in Germany.

2.2.3.1.1.3.1. Ante Bruno, son of Mate (1937–1989)

Dr Bruno was born on 7 October 1937 in Ričice. In 1968, he married Vanja, née Brkljača. They had a son, Zvonimir, and two daughters, Zrinka and Tomislava.

Dr Ante Bruno was a Croatian physician, a member of the Parliament of the Socialist Republic of Croatia and a participant in the Croatian Spring. He was elected to Parliament in 1969, and after the suppression of the Croatian Spring, his parliamentary immunity was revoked.

Because of his political activities, he was arrested and sentenced to two years and four months of imprisonment under a strict regime, which he served at Stara Gradiška Prison.

2.2.3.1.1.3.1.1. Zvonimir, son of Ante Bruno (1973– )

Zvonimir married Aneta, née Gacek. They have a son, Oskar. The family lives in Supetar on the island of Brač.

2.2.3.1.1.3.2. Frane, son of Mate (1947– )

Mate’s younger son, Frane, married Matija, née Topalušić. They had two sons, Mate and Ante (1976–), and a daughter, Elza (1978–). They live in Ričice.

2.2.3.1.1.3.2.1. Mate, son of Frane (1973– )

Mate married Ana Marija, née Vlahović. They have two sons, Ante and Frane, and a daughter, Leonarda. The family lives in Ričice.

2.2.3.1.1.4.1. Drago, son of Nikola (1952– )

Drago and his wife Zdenka, née Budimir, had two sons, Nikola (1976–) and Ivica (1978–). Their son Nikola Tandara is a Montfortian priest in Poland. The family lives in Ričice.

2.2.3.1.1.4.1.1. Ivica, son of Drago (1978– )

2.2.3.1.1.4.2. Jerko, son of Nikola (1953– )

Nikola’s third son, Jerko, married Stanka, née Racetin. They have a daughter, Ana (1985–). He has no male descendants. The family lives in Ričice.

THE LINE OF MARIJAN, SON OF LOVRO

2.2.3.1.2. Marijan, son of Lovro (1881–1939) – descendants’ nickname: LOVRIĆI

Marijan married Luca, née Parlov. They had four sons—Mijo, Marin, Petar and Ivan (1924–1925)—and four daughters: Matija (1907–), Ana (1910–), Iva (1918–2005) and Tomica (1921–). Ivan died shortly after birth.

2.2.3.1.2.1. Mijo, son of Marijan (1908– )

Marijan’s eldest son, Mijo, was married to Slavka, née Sabljić. They had nine children: five sons—Vinko, Ivan (1932–1932), Milan, Marijan and another Ivan (1950–1952)—and four daughters: Mila-Iva (1933–), Anica (1935–), Nada (1940–) and Maca (1947–). Only the sons Vinko, Milan and Marijan continued Mijo’s line. The descendants of Vinko and Milan now live in Ričice, Split, Zagreb, Austria and Germany.

Vinko married Mara Dragica, née Budimir. They had two sons, Milenko (1955–1956) and Svetko, and two daughters, Dragica (1957–) and Milosava (1959–).

2.2.3.2. Mijo, son of Grgo (1856–1933)

Grgo’s third son, Mijo, married Iva Tandara. They had seven children: four sons—Martin (1886–1905), the first Nikola (1889–), Mate (1892–) and the second Nikola (1902–)—and three daughters: Iva (1883–), the first Mara (1886–1891) and the second Mara (1894–). Martin died at the age of nineteen, while the first Nikola and Mate died as young children. No information about the second Nikola has been found apart from the record of his birth. There is no evidence that any of Mijo’s sons married or had children, so this family branch ended.
