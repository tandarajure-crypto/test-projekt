MATE’S BRANCH

Mate Tandara and Filipa née Kopač

Mate Tandara: ★ c. 1740  –  ✝ unknown

Wife Filipa née Kopač: ★ unknown  –  ✝ unknown

Mate Tandara was the fourth son of Ivan Tandara. Mate and Filipa had five children: a son, Ivan, and daughters Matija, Manda, Jaka and Cecilija. The male line continued exclusively through their son Ivan.

Ivan married twice. In his first marriage, to Pava Sičenica, he had a son, Ante, the only male heir of Mate’s line. In his second marriage, to Jela Parlov, he had a daughter, Ruža.

This branch is traditionally known by the nickname CESAROVIĆI. According to a preserved family story, some of Mate’s descendants performed agricultural work for a family of the Lažeta line. When members of the household and neighbours asked them what the food was like at the Lažeta home, they replied: “Like at the emperor’s table!”

It remains unclear whether the word cesar or sesar was originally used. Although both forms can be found on older gravestones and in oral tradition, most descendants of this branch are now known as CESAROVIĆI. The descendants of Mate’s branch are shown in the diagram below.

Instructions for using the diagram

  1. Enter the name of the family member, a year, code or another known detail in the search field, then press Find.
  2. The Expand all button opens the complete Mate’s branch and displays all entered descendants.
  3. The Collapse button closes the expanded parts of the diagram.
  4. The Up to 3 generations button displays the first three generations of the family branch.
  5. The Fit button adjusts the entire diagram to the available space on the screen.
  6. To find your family line, first locate a known family member and then follow the connecting lines toward that person’s ancestors and descendants.
  7. Because many people share the same first name, enter the person’s name, father’s name and mother’s name for a precise match. You may also add the surname Tandara, but a first name and surname alone are often not enough to identify one person; the search includes both parents’ names.
