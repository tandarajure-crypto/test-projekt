MATE BRANCH

Mate Tandara and Filipa Kopač

Mate Tandara (★ c. 1740) was married to Filipa Kopač.

They had a son, Ivan, and daughters Matija, Manda, Jaka and Cecilija. The male line continued through Ivan.

This family branch is known by the nickname Cesarovići. In the diagram every child has a personal code: daughters use K, while sons without descendants or without a continuing line use S.

How to use the diagram

  1. Search by name, spouse, year, place or code.
  2. Click a person’s box to open the details.
  3. Use Expand all and Collapse all to control descendant branches.
  4. Use +, −, 100% and Fit entire tree to control zoom.
  5. Full screen opens the diagram across the display; on mobile devices, pinch with two fingers to zoom.
